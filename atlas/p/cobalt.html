What Europe imports

In August 2026, the EU imported €26.5 m of cobalt mattes and unwrought cobalt from outside the EU, 24% of it from China.

China everywhere else outside the EU. Monthly value of EU imports, Cobalt mattes and unwrought cobalt. Source: Eurostat Comext.

Why it matters for Europe

Finland is Europe's cobalt hub: Umicore refines it at Kokkola and Terrafame makes cobalt sulphate from its own ore at Sotkamo. China is the world's leading cobalt refiner.
