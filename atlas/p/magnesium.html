What Europe imports

In August 2026, the EU imported €12.8 m of unwrought magnesium, 99.8% or more from outside the EU, 94% of it from China.

China everywhere else outside the EU. Monthly value of EU imports, Unwrought magnesium, 99.8% or more. Source: Eurostat Comext.

Why it matters for Europe

The EU has no primary magnesium smelter. The Critical Raw Materials Act lists magnesium as strategic and aims to keep any single supplier below 65% by 2030.

Key figures

  • 86% of world primary magnesium was made in China in 2025 (950 of about 1,100 kt) USGS MCS 2026
  • 97% of the EU's primary magnesium comes from China; the EU has no smelter of its own SMM, from Eurostat data

Key questions

Which country controls magnesium?

What is magnesium used for in defence and space?

Titanium production (the Kroll process), infrared decoy flares, pyrotechnics and light alloys for airframes and armour.

Can Europe produce magnesium itself?
