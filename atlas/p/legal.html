Privacy

OreOrbit uses no cookies and no trackers. It counts page views and button clicks as anonymous daily totals, without storing IP addresses or anything else about visitors, and skips browsers that ask not to be tracked. Your settings (language, sound, chosen materials) stay in your own browser's storage on your device. The only thing ever sent is your email address, and only when you sign up for the Monday brief or monitoring (see below). Like every website, the hosting server keeps standard access logs for security and operation.

Email sign-ups

If you sign up for the free Monday brief or for monitoring, we process your email address, the minerals you chose, the product you checked (if any) and your language, to send you what you asked for. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to david@madali.cz. We keep this data until you unsubscribe. It is stored on our web hosting (WEDOS Internet, a.s., Czech Republic) and the emails are sent through its mail server; it is never sold or shared. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data and to complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

Monitoring subscription

Monitoring is a monthly subscription, paid by card in advance through Stripe. You can cancel anytime from the billing portal (link in every monitoring email, or manage your subscription); it then runs to the end of the paid month. Money-back guarantee: if you do not see the value, ask within the first month and we refund your payment in full. Alerts are sent when the atlas next updates, usually within a few hours of a change.

Terms of use

OreOrbit is provided free of charge for information. Data are collected automatically from public sources and can contain errors or delays; stories found automatically are not checked. Nothing here is investment, legal or professional advice. Check the original sources before relying on a figure.

OreOrbit's own text, figures and charts may be quoted and reused with credit to “OreOrbit (Bohemia Defense)” and a link. Third-party data remain under their own licences, listed below.
