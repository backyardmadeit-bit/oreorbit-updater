Lithium

Jadar

Potentially one of Europe's biggest lithium sources, and one of its most contested.

Material
Lithium
Stage
Planned project
Place
Loznica, Serbia
Operator
Rio Tinto
Coordinates
44.53° N, 19.22° E (approximate)
Expected online

Seen from orbit

Heat
0 of the last 7 days and 0 of the last 30 had furnace-hot pixels within 6 km (NASA FIRMS).

Signals show activity, not output. They are measured automatically and can be affected by weather, terrain and fires.

Facts

  • Jadarite, a lithium-boron mineral, is known only from here
  • Resource of 118 Mt at 1.8% lithium oxide
  • Licences revoked in January 2022 after protests; Serbia's Constitutional Court overturned that in July 2024
  • EU–Serbia raw-materials memorandum signed in July 2024
