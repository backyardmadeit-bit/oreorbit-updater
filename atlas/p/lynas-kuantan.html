Lynas Advanced Materials Plant

Refining is the bottleneck in rare earths, and this is one of the few large refineries outside China.

Facts

  • Described as the world's largest rare-earth extraction plant outside China
  • Target capacity 11,000 t of rare-earth oxides a year; operating since 2012
  • Malaysia ordered its cracking and leaching operations stopped in 2023
