  • Week 1Two working sessions with your engineering and purchasing teams to fill the register, part by part.
  • Week 2Analysis and stress tests against export controls, chokepoints and sites, using OreOrbit's live data.
  • Week 3The report and a presentation for management. You keep the register.

Your data

Your register stays yours. We sign a non-disclosure agreement on request, the register can be built in your browser so that it never leaves your premises, and nothing about your company is ever published. Anonymised, aggregated figures are only used with your written consent.

What it is, and what it is not

A review is an independent analysis of supply-chain risk, from public data and the information you provide. It supports the documentation the Critical Raw Materials Act asks for, but it is not legal advice or a certification; confirm your legal obligations with counsel.
