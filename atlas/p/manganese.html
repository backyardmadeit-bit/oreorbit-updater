What Europe imports

In August 2026, the EU imported €23.5 m of manganese metal and articles from outside the EU, 85% of it from China.

China everywhere else outside the EU. Monthly value of EU imports, Manganese metal and articles. Source: Eurostat Comext.

Why it matters for Europe

Europe has no operating manganese mine. Chvaletice in the Czech Republic, an EU strategic project, plans battery-grade manganese from old tailings, with a feasibility study due in the first half of 2027.

Key questions

Which country controls manganese?

China produces 95% of the world's electrolytic manganese metal. Source: SMM, 2026.

What is manganese used for in defence and space?

Steel for armour, shells and vehicles (about 90% of all manganese) and battery cathodes for drones and soldier power.

Can Europe produce manganese itself?

Europe has no operating manganese mine. Chvaletice in the Czech Republic, an EU strategic project, plans battery-grade manganese from old tailings, with a feasibility study due in the first half of 2027.
