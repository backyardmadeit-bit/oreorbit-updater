Kalahari Manganese Field

The largest manganese source on Earth.

Material
Manganese
Stage
Mine
Place
Hotazel, Northern Cape, South Africa
Coordinates
27.20° S, 22.95° E (approximate)

Seen from orbit

Heat
1 of the last 7 days and 1 of the last 30 had furnace-hot pixels within 6 km (NASA FIRMS).

Signals show activity, not output. They are measured automatically and can be affected by weather, terrain and fires.

Facts

  • South Africa mined 7.6 of the world's 20 Mt of manganese in 2025 (USGS)
  • Holds about a third of world reserves
