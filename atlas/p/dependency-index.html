How it is made

  • Magnesium and manganese, added in November 2026, are tracked separately so the index stays comparable over time.
  • For each of the ten index minerals, OreOrbit adds up the value of the EU's monthly imports from outside the EU of the products it tracks (for example permanent magnets, rare-earth compounds and metals for rare earths) and takes China's share.
  • The index is the plain average of the ten shares, so a small but critical mineral such as gallium counts as much as lithium.
  • Values, not tonnes; imports from outside the EU only. Single months can swing widely when few shipments arrive, so read the trend more than one month.
  • Updated automatically when Eurostat publishes a new month, usually about six weeks after the month ends.
