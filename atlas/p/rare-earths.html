Why it matters for Europe

Europe mines almost no rare earths. Estonia is the exception on the processing side: separation in Sillamäe and Europe's largest magnet plant in Narva.

Key figures

  • 69% of world rare-earth mine output came from China in 2024 (270,000 of about 390,000 t) USGS MCS 2025
  • 44 Mt of the world's 90+ million tonnes of reserves are in China USGS MCS 2025
