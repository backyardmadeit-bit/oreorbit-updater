What Europe imports

In August 2026, the EU imported €15.5 m of unwrought titanium (sponge) from outside the EU, 11% of it from China.

China everywhere else outside the EU. Monthly value of EU imports, Unwrought titanium (sponge). Source: Eurostat Comext.

Why it matters for Europe

Norway's Tellnes is one of Europe's largest titanium mines. Ukraine's ilmenite deposits at Irshansk and Vilnohirsk are a strategic asset; Irshansk restarted in 2023 after a war-related shutdown.

Key figures

  • 67% of world titanium sponge output came from China in 2023 USGS SIR 2025-5021
  • 300+ kt of titanium sponge was produced worldwide in 2024, up from about 200 kt in 2018 USGS SIR 2025-5021
