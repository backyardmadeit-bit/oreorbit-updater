IMIP Morowali

The heart of Indonesia's nickel boom, built largely with Chinese capital.

Material
Nickel
Stage
Processing plant
Place
Central Sulawesi, Indonesia
Operator
Tsingshan-led industrial park

Radar
Latest Sentinel-1 radar picture: 30 Oct 2026.

Signals show activity, not output. They are measured automatically and can be affected by weather, terrain and fires.

Facts

  • About $34.3 billion invested from 2015 to 2024
  • About 85,000 workers
  • Runs largely on captive coal power, including about 2 GW of coal plant
  • Indonesia mined about 2.2 of the world's 3.7 Mt of nickel in 2024 (USGS)
